# 📊 Unitee

A lifestyle T-shirt brand focused on modern, minimal, and story-driven apparel.

---

## 💡 Project Idea

**Unitee** is an independent clothing brand that creates high-quality, minimal, and expressive T-shirts designed for young people who value style, identity, and meaning.
The goal is to build a unified brand experience — **“Unitee”** — that connects design, quality, and personal expression through compelling visuals and meaningful concepts.

The project focuses on:

* Developing a complete **brand identity** (logo, colors, tone, visual style).
* Producing **visual content** for social media (photos, reels, stories, campaigns).
* Building strong concepts for each T-shirt collection with **storytelling**.
* Showcasing the **production journey**: Idea → Design → Printing → Packaging.
* Highlighting **fabric quality and craftsmanship** through professional shoots.
* Building an engaged **community** around lifestyle, fashion, and creativity.

## 🗂️ Work Plan

### **1. Research & Analysis**
* Market research for local and global T-shirt trends.
* Competitor analysis (streetwear, minimal brands).
* Audience personas (students, young adults, fashion-driven buyers).
* Moodboards and creative direction.

### **2. Brand Identity**
* Logo design and brand naming.
* Color palette and typography system.
* Tone of voice (Minimal – Confident – Youthful).
* Visual guidelines for apparel and content.

### **3. Awareness Content**
* Product photography and mockups.
* Lifestyle shoots (indoor/outdoor).
* **Reels production:** Behind the scenes, printing process, and packaging.
* Awareness campaigns introducing Unitee’s concept and identity.

### **4. Product & Collections**
* Designing graphics for each collection or drop.
* Creating high-quality mockups before production.
* “Concept to T-shirt” video content.
* Writing product descriptions highlighting quality, fabric, and meaning.

### **5. Community Building**
* Growing an active community on Instagram and TikTok.
* User-generated content (customers showcasing their T-shirts).
* Giveaways to increase brand awareness.
* Collecting suggestions for future designs.

### **6. Review & Finalization**
* Reviewing identity, content, and product quality.
* Preparing a complete brand portfolio.
* Uploading project files to Drive / GitHub.
* Final presentation setup.
